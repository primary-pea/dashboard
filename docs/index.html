        Every way we could have cut it anaemia

        336 defensible specifications — seven ways of counting the dose, three lags, two income measures, with and without context controls, four estimators — each re-fitted and sorted by its estimate. Filter with the chips; hover any dot for its recipe. A dot that clears the zero line is a specification whose interval excludes no effect.

        Where the clusters come from

        Every GIFNA record carries controlled-vocabulary tokens. Each token is matched, case-insensitively, against the 47 patterns below — never against free text — and a record joins every cluster its tokens hit. Unmatched tokens are reported, not guessed. The chosen cluster is outlined; the counts are countries with at least one active record in 2023 and the mean stock.
